# Team and Agent Roster

Create a role only when recurring work, clear inputs, and a verifiable output
justify it. One capable generalist is better than several decorative agents.

| Role | Type | Mission | Inputs | Outputs | Authority | Escalates to | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Project steward | human | Own intent and consequential decisions | Project evidence | Priorities and approvals | Final project decisions | — | active |
| [UNKNOWN] | agent | [UNKNOWN] | [UNKNOWN] | [UNKNOWN] | [UNKNOWN] | Project steward | proposed |

## Handoff contract

Every handoff should state:

1. requested outcome;
2. evidence and source paths;
3. constraints and permissions;
4. expected artifact;
5. acceptance criteria;
6. unresolved questions.
