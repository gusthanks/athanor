# Glossary

- **Athanor:** the installable project operating-system toolkit.
- **Nigredo:** read-only discovery and diagnosis.
- **Albedo:** consolidation of structure and sources of truth.
- **Citrinitas:** design of agents, handoffs, and capabilities.
- **Rubedo:** execution, integration, preservation, and maintenance.
- **Sigil:** the project's condensed intent and durable operating rules.
- **Gnosis:** verified knowledge and memory.
- **Circle:** scope, permissions, and risk boundaries.
- **Ritual:** repeatable workflow.
- **Correspondence:** a mapping between outcome, owner, source, and capability.
- **Chronicle:** Git, GitHub, history, and preservation workflow.
- **Solve et Coagula:** plan-first organization workflow.
- **Git:** local version history.
- **GitHub:** remote hosting and collaboration for Git repositories.
- **Git LFS:** pointer-based handling of selected large files.
- **Backup:** recoverable copy independent of active workspace state.
- **MCP:** protocol that exposes tools and data to compatible AI clients.
- **API:** programmatic interface to a service.
- **Agent:** a bounded role with sources, tools, constraints, and handoffs.
- **Memory:** curated cross-session knowledge; never a substitute for current
  evidence.
