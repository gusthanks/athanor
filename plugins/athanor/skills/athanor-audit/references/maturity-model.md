# Maturity Model

## Domains

Assess only domains relevant to the project:

1. identity and purpose;
2. stakeholders and decision authority;
3. source hierarchy and documentation;
4. product, service, curriculum, or creative output;
5. audience, positioning, and brand;
6. design system and reusable assets;
7. operations and file organization;
8. marketing, sales, and customer support;
9. legal, privacy, intellectual property, and regulation;
10. finance and administrative controls;
11. technology, data, testing, and deployment;
12. Git, backup, preservation, and disaster recovery;
13. tools, integrations, and automation;
14. agent ownership, handoffs, and review;
15. memory, decisions, tasks, and drift maintenance.

## States

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| unknown | Evidence has not been inspected |
| absent | Evidence confirms the capability is missing |
| fragmented | Pieces exist without a canonical owner |
| documented | A usable description exists |
| canonical | One maintained source of truth exists |
| operational | The capability is used, verified, and maintained |

## Scoring restraint

Do not produce a vanity percentage. A launch-blocking privacy gap can matter
more than ten polished documents. Prioritize by current objective, impact,
likelihood, reversibility, and dependency.
